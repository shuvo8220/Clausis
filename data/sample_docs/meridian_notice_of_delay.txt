Pursuant to Section 12.3 of the Construction Services Agreement dated September 1, 2022 
(the "Agreement"), Meridian Construction Group, Inc. hereby provides formal notice of 
a delay event affecting the above-referenced project.

NATURE OF DELAY

Beginning in November 2022, Meridian experienced severe disruptions to its supply 
chain affecting the following critical path items:

1. HVAC units (Carrier model 50XC-Series): 16-week backorder from manufacturer
2. Electrical switchgear panels (Cutler-Hammer Distribution Series): 12-week delay
3. Elevator components (OTIS Gen2 system): 20-week supply disruption

These delays were caused by ongoing global semiconductor shortages and post-pandemic 
supply chain instability, circumstances entirely outside Meridian's control or 
reasonable anticipation at the time of contract execution.

CONTRACTUAL BASIS

Article 14 of the Agreement provides for force majeure relief in the event of delays 
caused by circumstances beyond the reasonable control of the contractor, including 
material shortages. Meridian invokes this provision as justification for the delay.

Meridian requests a contract extension of not less than one hundred twenty (120) 
calendar days from the original substantial completion date of March 1, 2023.

CURRENT PROJECT STATUS (as of February 18, 2023)

Completed:
- Structural work: 100%
- Exterior facade: 100%
- Floors 1 and 2 interior buildout: 95%
- Plumbing (all floors): 88%

Pending (awaiting materials):
- HVAC installation: 40% (equipment on order)
- Electrical: floors 3-4: 30% (switchgear pending)
- Elevator modernization: 15% (components in transit)

RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

Meridian expressly reserves all rights under the Agreement, including but not limited to 
claims for additional compensation for extended general conditions costs incurred as a 
result of the delay, which are estimated at $12,500.00 per month.
