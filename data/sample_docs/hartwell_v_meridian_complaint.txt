GENERAL ALLEGATIONS

1. On or about September 1, 2022, Hartwell and Meridian entered into a written 
Construction Services Agreement ("Agreement") for the renovation of Hartwell's 
commercial property located at 800 South Flower Street, Los Angeles, CA 90017 
(the "Property").

2. The total contract price was $2,450,000.00. Hartwell paid an initial deposit of 
$490,000.00 (20%) on September 15, 2022, per Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

3. The Agreement required substantial completion by March 1, 2023, per Section 6.1.

4. Meridian failed to achieve substantial completion by the contractual deadline. As of 
the date of this filing, the project remains incomplete, with critical systems including 
HVAC, electrical on floors 3 and 4, and elevator modernization unfinished.

5. Hartwell has suffered damages including: lost rental income of approximately 
$180,000.00 per month since March 2023, costs of $87,500.00 for emergency 
temporary repairs, and additional consultant fees of $34,200.00.

6. Meridian issued a purported Notice of Delay on February 20, 2023, citing supply 
chain disruptions. Hartwell disputes the validity of this notice as it was not served 
in the manner required by Section 12.3 of the Agreement.

---

CAUSES OF ACTION

FIRST CAUSE OF ACTION - Breach of Contract
Plaintiff incorporates all foregoing allegations. Meridian materially breached the 
Agreement by failing to complete the work within the agreed timeframe and failing 
to perform work in a workmanlike manner as required by § 5.1 of the Agreement.

SECOND CAUSE OF ACTION - Negligence  
Meridian owed Plaintiff a duty of care in performing construction services. Meridian 
breached this duty through substandard workmanship, causing damages in an amount 
to be proven at trial.

THIRD CAUSE OF ACTION - Fraud (against Does 1-20)
Plaintiff is informed and believes that certain Doe defendants made material 
misrepresentations regarding the project timeline and materials used.

PRAYER FOR RELIEF

Plaintiff prays for judgment as follows:

1. Compensatory damages in an amount to be proven at trial, estimated at no less
